- **FACT:** no prior version implements the complete hinted contract: `TF-IDF + BM25 + dense retrieval + label-safe template/train-neighbor statistics + candidate-specific features → LightGBM fusion`.
- **Biggest V50–V127 evidence change:** V127 turns dual E5/Nomic Top1 selection from “not executed” into a small, positive nested result (`+0.0007095`, cluster CI `[+0.000167,+0.001324]`, all folds positive), while the leader audit invalidates apparent large wins from V98 and V104 and shows V124/V125 did not test their stated hypotheses correctly.

Latest skeleton coverage is high (`81.6%` of test), yet repeated latest-skeleton groups have only `40.0%` modal Top1 accuracy and `19.4%` modal Top5-set accuracy in train.

Reaching `>0.66193` (`+0.00165`) is a credible stretch only if the hybrid’s gain survives a prospective lock and transfers to the exact champion action geometry.
- The `0.96609` result is mathematically possible, but visible exact/near-template stability does not support a near-perfect mapping by itself. It remains a clue to test, not a target from which to reverse-engineer labels or infer misconduct.

## 4. Fresh EDA Findings

**Chat and template geometry.** There are 150 competition users without chat—117 train and 33 test—and 90 extra chat users outside train/test that must never contribute supervised statistics. Message counts are concentrated but not single-turn: median `2`, 90th percentile `6`, maximum `10`; train/test means are `2.870/2.904`, with KS `0.030`.

Normalization used Unicode NFKC, HTML unescaping, lowercasing, URL replacement, punctuation-to-space, and whitespace collapse. A target-free skeleton additionally replaced recognized catalog aliases and integers. Coverage and stability are very different notions:

| View | Test key seen in train | Repeated-train coverage, excluding no-chat | Modal Top1 / Top5-set within repeated train keys |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Latest exact | 48.2% (`46.4%` excluding no-chat) | 44.9% | 55.6% / 35.5% |
| Full-history exact | 27.4% (`24.9%`) | 21.6% | 57.4% / 29.6% |
| Latest skeleton | 81.6% (`81.0%`) | 78.6% | 40.0% / 19.4% |
| Full-history skeleton | 34.2% (`32.0%`) | 30.1% | 42.5% / 18.1% |

At character-TFIDF nearest-history similarity, the median test-to-train maximum is `0.8933`; coverage is `90.6%` at `0.80`, `70.6%` at `0.85`, `47.8%` at `0.90`, and `28.2%` at `0.99`. Single-link clustering at `0.90` yields 2,838 clusters; 48.3% of test users share a cluster with train. Yet those near clusters have only `37.4%` modal Top1 and `13.4%` modal Top5-set accuracy. **SUPPORTED INFERENCE:** templates create useful priors, not answers.

Language is stateful. Broad deterministic patterns find comparison language in 265 train users, cancellation in 101, and preference wording in 2,101. Among 1,310 multi-message users whose first and last messages each uniquely name different modules, true Top1 matches the first module `19.9%` and last `36.8%`; Top5 includes them `58.5%` and `74.2%`. In 78 clearer cancellation transitions, the last module is Top1 only `35.9%`, a prior-mentioned module is Top1 `48.7%`, and the last beats every prior module `37.2%`. Recency matters, but “latest overwrites history” is false. The alias detector is deliberately conservative and misses literal families such as M004/M005/M011/M013, so mention counts are lower bounds.

Latest-skeleton train/test frequency divergence is JS `0.349` bits and total variation `0.469`; many rare categories inflate these figures. A label-free adversarial classifier is near chance: AUC `0.513` under random folds and `0.522` under latest-template groups. Thus there is no evidence of a large marginal shift, but conditional target shift remains possible.

**Ranking geometry.** Positive-set sizes are `K=4: 1,362`, `K=5: 1,285`, `K=6: 1,353`. Sorted relevance means are `[1.0000, 0.8492, 0.7000, 0.5491, 0.2637, 0.0847]`; rank 5 is zero for `34.05%` and rank 6 for `66.18%`. Top1 concentrates in M003 (`12.38%`), M002 (`10.48%`), M006 (`10.03%`), M012 (`9.40%`), and M009 (`8.85%`); M004/M011/M013 are rare (`0.70%/1.00%/1.73%`). Per-module modeling is plausible, but rare blocks are noisy.

Exact oracle swap leverage, averaged over all train users, is Top1 `0.02509`, B23 `0.00879`, B34 `0.00471`, B45 `0.00575`, and rank5-versus-rank6 membership `0.03040` (nonzero for `65.95%`). These are diagnostic upper levers, not model gains. As scale only, `+0.00065` over 1,000 users equals about 26 perfectly corrected mean Top1 swaps with no harm; `+0.00165` equals about 66. Real policies need more actions because precision is below one.

Global precedence is not deterministic: the strongest pair with support at least 100 is M003 over M004 at `76.1%`; common pairs sit near `50%`. Within full-history skeleton strata, weighted direction confidence is `72.6%`; adding coarse assessment raises it to `75.7%` but cuts usable observations sharply. **SUPPORTED INFERENCE:** module-specific nonlinear interactions are more promising than a single global curriculum order.

**Assessments.** Of 50,000 cells, 748 are strings and 251 are invalid/out of range, affecting 73 users; train/test counts are 188/63 cells and 53/20 users. In the pilot, correctly parsed assessment features add roughly `+0.012` to a simple text Ridge in every split world, including contiguous ID. This is not new champion residual evidence—the champion already uses assessment—but confirms that retrieval fusion must condition on readiness rather than discard it.

**Validation regimes.** A simple sparse Ridge scores `0.64095` random, `0.63637` exact-history-grouped, `0.63573` latest-skeleton-grouped, `0.63589` near-template-grouped, and `0.63969` contiguous-ID. ## 5. The 0.96609 Hint and the Missing Retrieval Question

**FACT:** exhaustive notebook-source parsing found no `BM25`, `rank_bm25`, import, class, or scoring implementation through V127. **FACT:** no version has the full hinted feature-to-fusion contract. BM25 is a term-saturation and document-length-normalized lexical ranker, not another name for TF-IDF; its probabilistic retrieval lineage is described by [Robertson and Zaragoza](https://www.nowpublishers.com/article/DownloadEBook/INR-019).

Prior work misses different pieces. V3.1’s per-module LightGBM calibrates sparse features without BM25, dense retrieval, or neighbor target evidence. V20 probes BGE-M3 modes but does not form the full supervised candidate table. V30/V31 use anchor and curriculum geometry, not lexical+dense retrieval fusion. V33 retrieves action analogues, not user/module and user/user evidence across BM25, TF-IDF, and dense spaces. V49 blends historical predictions. V62’s per-module LightGBM (`0.6271` versus Ridge `0.6282`) receives raw sparse/assessment features and acts as a replacement; it lacks retrieval ranks, margins, disagreements, and conservative residual geometry. V127 uses E5/Nomic action heads but no BM25, train-user statistics, or LightGBM fusion.

The fresh pilot makes the clue actionable but narrows its interpretation. BM25 user-to-module NDCG (`0.4991`) is slightly below word TF-IDF (`0.5054`). As train-user kNN it beats TF-IDF kNN (`0.5371` versus `0.5274`) but remains about `0.10` below the sparse anchor. Direct BM25 blending is flat/negative. Conversely, label-free retrieval score/rank/margin features inside per-module LightGBM add `+0.0041` to `+0.0048` at 15% residual weight across three split worlds, with positive user and template-cluster CIs and positive folds. This pilot excluded dense features and target-derived neighbor statistics, and the strong-anchor comparison was post-hoc against a simplified anchor; it establishes a reason to run V128, not a reason to submit.

Could retrieval explain `0.96609`? High test coverage—especially `81.6%` latest-skeleton and `90.6%` near-history at similarity `0.80`—allows a benchmark-faithful seen-template mechanism to matter. But observed target entropy, weak template modal accuracy, and poor raw neighbor models reject a near-deterministic visible-template mapping. A genuine leap would require interactions that recover hidden slots such as readiness, module identity, recency, and lexical/dense disagreement far better than existing models.

The non-accusatory discriminator is prospective evidence: target-free cluster construction; outer-fold-only neighbor labels; a frozen lock opened once; matched seen/unseen results; target-shuffle and self-neighbor negative controls; stable module blocks; identical OOF/deployment code; exact cache/input hashes; organizer reproducibility; and ultimately independent/private performance. Reused grouped OOF and OOB may legitimately describe different regimes, but two methods cannot be compared causally across them. Dual evaluation is required, with seen/unseen membership determined only from each outer training pool.

## 6. What V127 Actually Established

V127 reconstructs a compact champion-like anchor inside five grouped outer folds. For each user it considers swapping the anchor Top1 score value with ranks 2, 3, or 4. Exact counterfactual NDCG labels train three-action Ridge heads. One head is an anchor/assessment control; E5 and Nomic heads add normalized user/module coordinate interactions. A full action requires both encoder heads to select the same challenger and exceed an inner-selected threshold. Top5 membership and rank5 stay fixed.

The direct result is FULL `+0.0007095365`, cluster CI `[+0.0001670634,+0.0013238589]`, worst fold `+0.00009467`, and 169/4,000 OOF changes. CONTROL is only `+0.00009110`, CI crossing zero, worst fold `-0.0004823`, and 38 changes. **SUPPORTED INFERENCE:** semantic representations contain incremental action information. It is not clean causal attribution because FULL and CONTROL have different heads, consensus rules, thresholds, and action counts; a matched-action-rate ablation is still needed.

The gate failed for two direct reasons: `POOLED_FLOOR=0.002` rejects `0.00071`, and the minimum anchor-Top1 module block is `-0.001581`. The CI and all fold deltas themselves pass. The stored dominant block `[2,+0.001500]` is zero-based in code, so it maps to M003; prose calling it M002 is an off-by-one display bug. The minimum block identity was not persisted and is **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

V127 is not an exact champion comparison. Its own source documents missing full intent-state/Hybrid34/B-state behavior, compact assessments, evaluation on all train users, and only eight protected V25 test users rather than the complete S0 state. Test action rate is `57/1000=5.7%`, about 35% higher than OOF’s `4.225%`, and materially broader than the V115/V126 public-adjacent packages. Its local E5/Nomic caches also lack full provenance.

Therefore V127 should be reused as tested infrastructure and a semantic feature family, not as a submission policy. Keep its exact utility/geometry assertions, nested fit audit, prefixes (`query:`/`passage:` for E5; `search_query:`/`search_document:` for Nomic), and fail-close writer. Replace its compact comparator and standalone semantic authority with the V128 hybrid evidence and full protection mask. E5’s retrieval-oriented contrastive design is documented in its [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03533); Nomic v2’s multilingual MoE details and usage are in its [official model card](https://huggingface.co/nomic-ai/nomic-embed-text-v2-moe).

## 7. Why We Are Still at 0.66028

Ranked bottlenecks are:

1. **Authority calibration and deployment parity.** Many local gains change different users/scores than the submitted package, use an easier comparator, or select thresholds repeatedly on the same labels.
2. **Under-modeled conditional interaction.** Sparse, dense, BM25, template, assessment, module, and anchor signals are individually imperfect; the complete candidate-specific nonlinear fusion was never tested.
3. **Template ambiguity.** High coverage coexists with high within-template target entropy, so lookup and generic semantic similarity cannot order related modules reliably.
4. **Small, heterogeneous action support.** Rare modules and low-coverage policies make module blocks noisy; a pooled gain can hide harm.
5. **Historical validation contamination.** All 4,000 labels have influenced many choices. A reshuffle is not pristine, and public scores have also become development feedback; model-selection bias can rival algorithm differences, as discussed by [Cawley and Talbot](https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v11/cawley10a.html).

Genuinely closed mechanisms are unconstrained whole-list dense replacement, raw TF-IDF/E5/BM25 neighbor voting, latest-only routing, naive template popularity, generic objective changes, lower-boundary surgery without a strong utility model, leaky self-training, V98-style OOB bagging evidence, and unmatched no-chat overrides. They may be components or negative controls, but should not receive another standalone version.

## 9. Selected Immediate Next Architecture

Call the next notebook **V128-Astra Cross-Fitted Hybrid Retrieval-to-Action Fusion**. It should produce OOF artifacts and a fail-closed candidate CSV, not assume adoption.

**Data views.** Sort chat by `(user_id, timestamp, chat_id)`. Normalize with `html.unescape`, NFKC, casefold, URL→`<url>`, punctuation→space, and collapsed whitespace; retain negation words. Store (1) latest message, (2) chronological full history joined with `[SEP]`, and (3) message-level texts. Recency retrieval is the normalized weighted average of message scores with weights `1, 1/2, 1/4, ...` backward from the latest. No-chat text is `<no_chat>` plus an explicit flag.

Parse the assessment JSON in its catalogued ten-key order. Casefold `nol/satu/dua/tiga/empat/lima` to `0..5`; accept numeric values only if finite integral `0..5`; otherwise mark invalid and impute that user’s median valid response, falling back to the fold-training median. Include ten cleaned values, ten invalid masks, invalid count, mean/std/min/max, and candidate-module readiness differences. This exactly blocks the V115/V126 extrapolation bug.

Construct each module document only from organizer fields: `module_name [SEP] description_and_syllabus [SEP] prerequisite_level`. Derive aliases deterministically from names and acronyms plus unlabeled chat vocabulary; freeze the list before reading targets. Exact keys use normalized latest/full text. Skeleton keys additionally replace frozen module aliases with `<topic>` and integer runs with `<num>`. Near-template clusters use full-history `char_wb(3,5)` cosine single-link at `0.90`, built without labels; record the threshold and cluster hash.

The long table has one row per `(user,module)` and fixed feature order: user and module IDs; anchor score/rank, Top1 gap, rank-5 margin; each retriever’s score, rank, gap-to-best, gap-to-rank5; pairwise retriever disagreements; assessments/readiness; chat count/no-chat/recency flags; template/neighbor support and entropy; and incumbent/challenger contrasts for action evaluation. Assert exactly 17 distinct module rows per user and within-user feature variation.

**Sparse retrieval.** In every outer fold fit vectorizers on outer-training user texts plus the 17 fixed module documents, then transform validation. Use word TF-IDF `(1,2)`, `min_df=2`, sublinear TF, smooth IDF, L2 normalization, and `max_features=60,000`; use `char_wb(3,5)`, `min_df=2`, L2, `max_features=80,000`. Produce latest, full, and recency user→module cosine scores plus ranks and margins. The limits are conservative, fixed before LOCK, and may be lowered only for runtime—not tuned on LOCK.

Implement actual Okapi BM25 in NumPy/SciPy rather than adding a package: Unicode word tokens from `[\w]+`, preserving `sql`, `ai`, numbers, and Indonesian negation; `k1=1.2`, `b=0.75`, IDF `log(1+(N-df+0.5)/(df+0.5))`. Variant 1 uses unigrams; variant 2 adds adjacent underscore bigrams. Score latest/full/recency queries against the 17 module documents. Build a second BM25 index over outer-training full histories and retrieve top 20 train users. These two corpora answer different questions and must not be conflated.

**Dense retrieval.** Reuse exactly V127’s frozen models before considering checkpoints: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-base` with user `query:` and module/train document `passage:`; `nomic-ai/nomic-embed-text-v2-moe` with `search_query:` and `search_document:`. Mean-pool with attention mask, L2-normalize float32 outputs, and compute user→module cosine/rank/margin plus top-20 user neighbors and E5–Nomic/sparse–dense disagreement. Embeddings are label-free and may be cached once, but cache acceptance requires exact ordered-text SHA256, user/module order, model ID and immutable revision, tokenizer/config hashes, prefix, pooling, shape/dtype, package versions, and a re-encoded probe cosine above `0.999`.

**Fold-safe neighbor statistics.** For every outer validation user, all exact, skeleton, TF-IDF, BM25, E5, and Nomic neighbors come only from the outer-training pool. For fusion-training rows, generate the same statistics by inner cross-fitting; never let a row retrieve itself or labels from its held-out inner fold. For each space and module compute similarity-weighted relevance, Top1 probability, membership probability, top-20 support, support above `0.80/0.90/0.95`, maximum/mean/gap similarity, and entropy. Smooth target vectors toward outer-training module priors with five pseudo-neighbors: `(weighted_sum + 5*prior)/(weight_sum+5)`. Zero-support rows receive the prior plus a zero-support flag. Label-derived priors, scalers, calibrators, and template tables obey the same boundary. Add self-neighbor, target-shuffle, and excluded-label poisoning assertions.

**Fusion and ranking.** Fit 17 LightGBM regressors, one per module, to graded relevance. Module identity is implicit and usefulness may differ by module; keep a global one-hot diagnostic. Fixed configuration: `objective=regression_l2`, `n_estimators=160`, `learning_rate=.03`, `num_leaves=15`, `max_depth=4`, `min_child_samples=80`, `subsample=.8`, `subsample_freq=1`, `colsample_bytree=.75`, `reg_alpha=.5`, `reg_lambda=8`, deterministic column-wise training, seeds `20260907, 20260917, 20260927`; average the seeds. Do not search a broad grid. Record the pilot environment (`numpy 2.5.3`, `pandas 3.0.5`, `scipy 1.18.1`, `scikit-learn 1.9.0`, `lightgbm 4.7.0`) but pin V128 to a smoke-tested offline wheelhouse.

Within each user, align the fused 17-vector to the reconstructed anchor’s mean and standard deviation. Stage-A score is `anchor + 0.15*(aligned_fusion-anchor)`, with `0.15` frozen from the pilot. For deployment, consider champion ranks 2–4. For challenger at rank `r`, set `u_hat=(f_challenger-f_incumbent)*(1/log2(2)-1/log2(r+1))/IDCG_hat`, where `IDCG_hat` is DCG@5 of the sorted, `[0,1]`-clipped fused relevance vector, floored at `0.1`. Inner OOF risk–coverage selects one threshold from a preregistered grid; choose the highest within one standard error of best mean exact delta. At most one positive-utility challenger is swapped with Top1.

Full V42/V30/V31 state and protection manifests must be reconstructed, not V127’s eight-user approximation. Test output starts from the exact champion hash and swaps existing score values only for allowed S0 users, preserving Top5 membership, rank5, row multiset, and every protected action. If any gate, provenance, finite-value, schema, or geometry assertion fails, copy champion bytes. Final training uses all 4,000 labels; test neighbor references are train only. Never load a prior pseudo-GT or submission as a target.

**Runtime.** Sparse/BM25 retrieval and 17 small LightGBMs run on CPU; compute user-neighbor similarities in chunks and retain only top 20. Encode E5 and Nomic once in float16 batches on the two T4s, serialize float32 normalized caches, then release GPU memory. V127’s recorded probe/syllabus/experiment path was about eight minutes excluding cache generation, so V128 is feasible if caches and sparse matrices are reused across folds. Vendor model snapshots and wheels as Kaggle datasets, disable network, set thread counts, and fail with a clear provenance error rather than silently changing models. Do not install the optional Nomic megablocks fork unless a smoke benchmark proves it necessary.

## 10. Validation and Falsification Plan

Create one target-blind prospective lock before implementation: a user enters `LOCK-P` when the first eight hex digits of `SHA256("AstraV128-20260907|" + user_id)` modulo 5 equal zero. Record IDs and hashes; do not inspect its labels until features, hyperparameters, action threshold rule, and ablations are frozen. The remainder is DESIGN. This lock is prospective only from V128 onward—historical work has already exposed all 4,000 labels.

On DESIGN, use identical users and predictions for every paired comparison in five worlds:

1. fixed five-fold random-user, stratified by Top1 and K;
2. full-history exact-template grouped;
3. latest-skeleton grouped;
4. target-free near-template cluster (`char_wb(3,5)`, threshold `0.90`) grouped;
5. contiguous-ID five-block diagnostics.

World 1 also reports seen/unseen slices where “seen” is defined only against that outer training pool. Form a benchmark-faithful mixture using the observed unlabeled test latest-skeleton coverage (`81.6% seen`, `18.4% unseen`). Worlds 2–4 measure family transfer and leakage sensitivity; they are stresses, not claims that test is entirely unseen-template. World 5 detects generator-batch sensitivity.

Primary comparisons are paired exact per-user NDCG@5: full hybrid versus the strongest reproducible champion analogue, no-BM25, no-target-neighbor, and V127-equivalent dense-only controls, at matched coverage and their own thresholds. On DESIGN, select full only if BM25 adds stable matched-coverage value; otherwise freeze no-BM25 before opening LOCK-P. Report pooled/fold deltas, user and near-template-cluster bootstrap CIs, action decomposition, changed users, module/template/no-chat blocks, and test/OOF action-rate ratio. Candidate A is Top1-only, so membership or lower-order changes are bugs.

Use a target-shuffled fusion, self-neighbor detector, and excluded-label poisoning run as mandatory negative controls. Dense cache features must be candidate-specific; within-user constant predictions fail. All preprocessing, neighbor construction, score alignment, thresholding, protection, and writing code must be shared between OOF and final deployment.

The promotion threshold is derived after uncertainty rather than copied from a historical `0.002` rule. Let `G_lock` be the once-opened LOCK-P paired gain, `G_mix` the test-coverage-weighted seen/unseen gain, `H` the larger half-width of the user and cluster 95% CIs on LOCK-P, and `S=max(0,G_design_selected-G_lock)` the observed selection-optimism penalty. Define `G_safe=min(G_lock,G_mix)-H-S`. A Top-5 candidate requires `G_safe >= 0.00065`; a credible #2 challenge requires `G_safe >= 0.00165`. All five outer-world pooled deltas must also be nonnegative, no fold worse than `-0.00025`, no module block with `n>=100` worse than `-0.001`, all geometry/provenance controls passing, and test action rate inside the 95% outer-fold interval.

**Strong NO-SUBMIT:** copy champion bytes if LOCK-P is nonpositive; either bootstrap lower bound is nonpositive; `G_safe < 0.00065`; the DESIGN-selected hybrid does not beat dense-only at matched coverage; full was selected without stable gain over no-BM25; one block dominates; any target boundary is violated; or deployment differs from evaluation. A single attractive random-fold score cannot override this.

## 11. Expected Gain and Risk

These are evidence brackets, not forecasts:

| Scenario | Evidence-consistent outcome | Competitive implication |
|---|---|---|
| Failure | BM25/neighbor features duplicate the champion, nested statistics lose their apparent value, or module blocks reverse; delta is zero/negative and output remains exact champion. | No leaderboard cost; close the full hinted branch as implemented. |
| Realistic | The hybrid retains a V127-scale selective gain around the current `+0.00065` gap, with roughly tens of high-confidence Top1 actions and no broad geometry change. | Could clear `0.66093`, but only the prospective gate makes that a defensible submission expectation. |
| Upside | Some portion of the pilot’s `+0.003–0.005` simplified-anchor residual survives the much stronger analogue and exact champion gate, with stable module blocks. | `+0.00165` and `>0.66193` become plausible; the broad residual fallback may then be worth one controlled test. |
| Extreme | Near-perfect retrieval/fusion approaches `0.96609`. | Unsupported by current entropy and neighbor evidence; do not budget or validate around it. |

The main statistical risk is selection optimism on 4,000 heavily reused labels. Main implementation risks are nested target-neighbor leakage, incomplete S0 reconstruction, cache/preprocessing drift, and per-module overfit—especially M004/M011/M013. Main distribution risk is that grouped and seen-template regimes weight different behavior. Main leaderboard risk is action volume: V127’s 57 test actions exceeded its OOF rate and its local gain only barely matches the Top-5 gap. The design therefore targets measured precision, not a fixed count.
